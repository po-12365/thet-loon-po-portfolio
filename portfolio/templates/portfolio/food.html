Ground Floor Café ☁

📍 Location:
103 Hunter St, Newcastle NSW 2300

Atmosphere:
Spacious, comfortable, and study-friendly

☕ My Favourite Order:
Cappuccino & spinach roll

Best For:
Studying, working on a laptop, and relaxing with coffee

My Experience:
I really enjoy the spacious interior, which makes it a great place to sit, study, or work on my laptop. Their cappuccino is delicious, and I also really enjoy their spinach roll. It is a perfect place for a productive coffee session.

View Menu 🍽️

Atmosphere:
Beautiful, stylish, spacious, and relaxing

☕ My Favourite Order:
Mocha and banana bread

Best For:
Dates, catching up with friends

My Experience:
Autumn Rooms is one of my favourite cafés in Newcastle because of its beautiful decoration and unique atmosphere. I really enjoy the upstairs area, which feels spacious and comfortable. The combination of beautiful design, good coffee, and a welcoming environment makes it a café I would happily recommend.

Visit Instagram 📸

Velvet latte
