The Journey That Shaped Me

I believe the best learning happens when curiosity meets action. Every experience has shaped the person I am today.

Age 18 · Vietnam 🇻🇳

Volunteered teaching children in Vietnam. This experience gave me confidence, independence, and helped me earn my parents' trust to begin building my own future.

Age 19 · Petroleum Engineering Degree at university

Studied in a male-dominated engineering field and received the Society of Petroleum Engineers (SPE) Scholarship Award.

Age 20 · Unexpected Turning Point

Political events back home interrupted my education. Looking back, this difficult chapter became the reason I discovered new opportunities abroad.

Age 21 · Australia 🇦🇺

Started a new life in Australia and began studying Business and Commerce while adapting to a completely new country.

Age 22 · Philippines 🇵🇭

Representing my community as a Youth Delegate at the University Youth Leadership Summit (UYLS) in the Philippines was a life-changing experience. Meeting passionate young leaders from around the world showed me that, despite our different backgrounds, we all share a common goal of creating positive change for our communities and protecting our planet.

Working Behind the Scenes

While studying, I worked in hospitality to support my living expenses and travel opportunities. Every early and late night shift reminded me that hard work creates opportunities, and every experience helps us grow. Working in a fast-paced environment taught me how to stay calm under pressure, manage stress, and remain focused even during the busiest moments. More importantly, it reminded me that everyone has their own story. Some customers may be having a difficult day, and a simple smile, warm greeting, or small act of kindness can make a meaningful difference.

Age 24 · Choosing Passion Over Comfort

One of the biggest decisions I made was changing my major from International Business to International Relations. I knew it might not be the easiest path into the job market, but I wanted to pursue something I genuinely cared about and could see myself making a meaningful contribution to in the future.

Future Diplomacy Forum · United States 🇺🇸

Participating in the Future Diplomacy Forum in the United States expanded my understanding of diplomacy, global affairs, and the growing influence of Artificial Intelligence in policy and international cooperation. It also inspired me to think about how technology and global leadership can work together to solve future challenges.

Student Leadership · University of Newcastle

I joined the UNSA and became actively involved in advocating for student welfare, diversity, and creating better opportunities for international students. Through conferences and leadership programs, I represented the voices of students and worked alongside others who shared the same passion for building a more inclusive community.

Professional Experience · Big Four Internship

I completed my placement and internship as an External Auditor at a Big Four accounting firm. This experience gave me valuable insight into professional workplace expectations and showed me the importance of balancing efficiency with attention to detail. It also taught me that strong technical skills are essential, but continuous learning and professionalism are equally important for building trust and delivering high-quality work.

Age 25 · Still Growing

This year has been one of the most rewarding chapters of my journey. I secured my graduate position, became Co-Chair of the NSW International Student Representative Committee. At the same time, I started my TikTok account to share travel experiences, study tips, workplace rights, tax knowledge, and life as an international student. More recently, I challenged myself to learn Python and build this portfolio from scratch, proving to myself that learning never truly stops.

My Reflection

Looking back, every challenge has become a lesson, every setback has created a new opportunity, and every person I have met has shaped who I am today. I truly believe that everything happens for a reason. Even when life doesn't go according to plan, there is always something to learn if we choose to keep moving forward. This journey is still being written, and I cannot wait to see where it takes me next.
