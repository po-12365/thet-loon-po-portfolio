Connecting the Dots

I started creating this portfolio with a simple goal: to learn the foundations of coding and understand how websites are built. At the beginning, seeing a simple message like “Hi, I’m Po” appear on the screen felt like a small achievement. However, that small moment helped me realise that every creative project begins with one small step. Once I understood the patterns behind coding and design, I became curious about what else I could create. I spent many nights and early mornings improving layouts, adjusting designs, solving problems, and finding better ways to structure my ideas. Every small improvement became a reminder that progress comes through patience and persistence. Seeing my project live on the internet was a moment I felt genuinely proud of. More importantly, this journey changed how I viewed myself. I used to believe that success came mainly from natural talent, and I often felt frustrated because I did not see myself as someone who was naturally gifted. Through this process, I learned that hard work is not something to be ashamed of. The hours, mistakes, and challenges are not signs of weakness; they are part of growth. Sometimes achieving a goal means spending days learning, rebuilding, and trying again. Looking back, each challenge became a star in my journey. Esach moment may seem small, but together they create a bigger picture of growth.
