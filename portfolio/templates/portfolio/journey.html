MY STORY | Thet Loon Po

The Journey
That Shaped Me

Every experience has shaped who I am today.
Every challenge became a lesson, and every opportunity became a new direction.

2018
EARLY FOUNDATION

Vietnam 🇻🇳

Volunteered teaching children in Vietnam. This experience gave me confidence, independence, and helped me begin building my own future.

2019

Petroleum Engineering

Studied in a male-dominated engineering field and received the Society of Petroleum Engineers Scholarship Award.

2020
TURNING POINT

Unexpected Turning Point

Political events back home interrupted my education. Looking back, this difficult chapter became the reason I discovered new opportunities abroad.

2022
NEW BEGINNING

Australia 🇦🇺

Started a new life in Australia and began studying Business and Commerce while adapting to a completely new country, culture, and environment.

A new country meant a new identity. I had to learn, adapt, and rebuild myself.

2023
GLOBAL LEADERSHIP

Philippines 🇵🇭

Representing my community as a Youth Delegate at the University Youth Leadership Summit (UYLS) in the Philippines was a life-changing experience. Meeting passionate young leaders from around the world showed me that, despite our different backgrounds, we share a common goal of creating positive change for our communities and protecting our planet.

WORKING BEHIND THE SCENES

The Lessons Outside the Classroom

While studying, I worked in hospitality to support my living expenses and travel opportunities. Every early morning and late night shift reminded me that hard work creates opportunities, and every experience helps us grow.

Working in a fast-paced environment taught me how to stay calm under pressure, manage stress, and remain focused during the busiest moments. More importantly, it reminded me that everyone has their own story. A simple smile, warm greeting, or small act of kindness can make a meaningful difference.

2024
A NEW DIRECTION

Choosing Passion Over Comfort

One of the biggest decisions I made was changing my major from International Business to International Relations. I knew it might not be the easiest path into the job market, but I wanted to pursue something I genuinely cared about and could see myself making a meaningful contribution in the future.

2025
GLOBAL PERSPECTIVE

Future Diplomacy Forum 🇺🇸

Participating in the Future Diplomacy Forum in the United States expanded my understanding of diplomacy, global affairs, and the growing influence of Artificial Intelligence in policy and international cooperation. It inspired me to think about how technology and global leadership can work together to solve future challenges.

2025
STUDENT LEADERSHIP

University of Newcastle

I joined the UNSA and became actively involved in advocating for student welfare, diversity, and creating better opportunities for international students. Through conferences and leadership programs, I represented the voices of students and worked alongside others who shared the same passion for building a more inclusive community.

2025/2026
PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

Big Four Internship

I completed my placement and internship as an External Auditor at a Big Four accounting firm. This experience gave me valuable insight into professional workplace expectations and showed me the importance of balancing efficiency with attention to detail. It taught me that strong technical skills are essential, but continuous learning and professionalism are equally important for building trust and delivering high-quality work.

2026
CONTINUING THE JOURNEY

Still Growing

This year has been one of the most rewarding chapters of my journey. I secured my graduate position and became Co-Chair of the NSW International Student Representative Committee. At the same time, I started my TikTok account to share travel experiences, study tips, workplace rights, tax knowledge, and life as an international student. More recently, I challenged myself to learn Python and build this portfolio from scratch, proving to myself that learning never truly stops.

MY REFLECTION

The Journey Is Still Being Written

Looking back, every challenge has become a lesson, every setback has created a new opportunity, and every person I have met has shaped who I am today.

I truly believe that every experience has a purpose. Even when life does not go according to plan, there is always something to learn if we choose to keep moving forward.

This journey is still being written, and I cannot wait to see where it takes me next.
